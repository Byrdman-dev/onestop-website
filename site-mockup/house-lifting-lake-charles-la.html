Common Questions

How high can a house be lifted?

The right height depends on your goals — flood protection, foundation access, or added space below — and will be discussed during your free inspection.

Do I need permits to lift my house?

Permit requirements vary by project and location. We'll go over what applies to your home during the inspection.

Can lifting be combined with foundation repair?

Yes — many lifting projects address foundation issues at the same time. We'll cover this as part of your free estimate.
