About One Stop Construction & Leveling

Locally owned and operated in Lake Charles, Louisiana — focused on house leveling, house lifting, and foundation repair done right the first time.

Our Approach

One Stop Construction & Leveling was built around a simple idea: fix the actual cause of a settling or damaged house, not just the symptoms. Every project starts with a free, honest inspection — not a sales pitch — so homeowners know exactly what's happening underneath their home before any work begins.

We use gentle, controlled air bag lifting instead of rushed jacking, because a house that's been settling for years shouldn't be forced back into place overnight. Every job is backed by a warranty, and every crew on site is covered by workers' comp — for our team's protection and yours.
