Option B — Bold Black Bar

Pro: Echoes the logo's own black-square badge directly, so the header and logo feel like one designed system; high contrast, premium/serious tone that fits structural repair work; white disc of the logo pops hard on black.

Con: Red-on-black only passes AA contrast at large/bold sizes (4.42:1, per logo contrast check) — the CTA text must stay bold and can't shrink much; an all-black bar can feel heavier/less "friendly" to a stressed homeowner than a lighter option.
