Option 4 — Full-Screen Minimal (Icon-Only)

Pro: The most compact mobile header — just the logo and two icon buttons (call, menu), no visible phone number taking up space. Tapping the call icon dials directly; tapping menu opens a bold full-screen overlay with large, easy-to-tap links.

Con: Hides the actual phone number from casual view — some visitors want to see/save the number before calling, not just tap-to-dial; the full-screen overlay is a bigger visual "mode switch" than a side drawer or dropdown.

Tap the menu icon below to open/close the full-screen overlay. Frame is sized to a typical phone width (390px).
