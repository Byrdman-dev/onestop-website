Option 1 — Slide-Out Drawer

Pro: The most familiar mobile nav pattern — a hamburger icon opens a full-height drawer from the side with a dimmed backdrop. Keeps the mobile header itself minimal (logo + hamburger + call icon only).

Con: Needs JS for the slide animation plus (later) focus-trap and escape-key handling for accessibility; nav links are hidden behind a tap rather than always visible.

Tap the hamburger icon below to open/close the drawer. Frame is sized to a typical phone width (390px) so the mobile pattern is clear regardless of your browser width.
