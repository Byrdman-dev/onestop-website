Option 1 — Pure Monochrome + Red Accent

Pro: Maximum brand consistency — nothing added beyond what's already in the logo. True neutral grays keep everything crisp and easy to read; red stays special because it's the only color, so it draws the eye straight to CTAs and key links every time.

Con: Can feel a little cold/corporate without a second hue to add warmth; every bit of visual interest beyond typography has to come from red, gray, and photography — more pressure on the before/after photos to carry the page.

All ratios below are computed WCAG 2.1 contrast ratios, not estimates.
