Option 3 — Steel Blue-Gray Secondary

Pro: Adds one secondary hue — a muted steel blue-gray — for things like secondary buttons, icons, and section backgrounds, so red can stay 100% reserved for primary CTAs and feel more urgent/special. Blue-gray reads as stable and structural, a reasonable fit for foundation/leveling work (steel beams, hydraulic jacks).

Con: This is the one option that introduces a color not derived from the logo itself — it needs its own contrast verification (done below) and adds a bit more palette to keep consistent across the site versus the simpler two-hue options.

All ratios below are computed WCAG 2.1 contrast ratios, not estimates.
