Option 2 — Warm Construction Neutrals

Pro: Same black/white/red core as the logo, but the gray scale is shifted slightly warm (toward tan/stone) instead of true neutral — evokes concrete, lumber, and job-site materials. Reads as more grounded and approachable than a cold gray, which can help with a stressed homeowner audience.

Con: Warm grays are easier to get slightly "muddy" if not tuned carefully (more exposure to that risk than true neutrals); slightly less crisp/clean-looking than Option 1 on a pure white background.
